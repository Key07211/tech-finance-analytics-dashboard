    Data & definitions
    • Snapshot. May 12, 2025 – May 11, 2026, 251 trading days, 35 stocks plus the S&P 500. The page uses the Close series saved in this repository (downloaded with yfinance; the download did not pin the adjustment convention, so dividends are not separately accounted for).
    • Return. Last Close ÷ first Close − 1. Rebased value = 100 × Closet ÷ Close0.
    • Theme path. Mean of the members' rebased values: equal money in each company at the start, then held with no rebalancing. The S&P 500 is a price index, not an investable product.
    • Drawdown. Close ÷ running high − 1; the maximum drawdown is its lowest point. Recovery is the first later day that regains the prior high; "not recovered" means through May 11, 2026 only. Days are calendar days.
    • Correlation. Pearson r on 250 aligned daily returns (Closet ÷ Closet−1 − 1). Pair averages weight every pair equally; the group figure correlates the equal-weight average daily returns of the 12 AI and 10 semiconductor stocks. Rolling values use the preceding 30 or 60 daily returns. Same-direction days count days when both returns have the same sign.
    • Boundaries. A personal watchlist, not an industry index. Historical description only: no forecasts, no causal claims, no trading signal.
