What does correlation mean?

Near +1: daily returns tend to line up. Near 0: little linear alignment. Near −1: they tend to move in opposite directions. A value of 0.50 does not mean “together on 50% of days.” Similar daily moves can still produce very different returns over a year.

07 / WHAT THIS YEAR LEFT US

Back to the original question.
What did the market show?

Three groups, each associated with expectations about the future, left different marks on this year.

A shared business story did not guarantee uniform stock-market behavior. Across AI–semiconductor pairs, average daily correlation was about 0.21; individual relationships varied, and group-average returns were more closely aligned.

Data sources, calculations & research boundaries

Snapshot: May 12, 2025–May 11, 2026, with 251 trading-day observations. Source: this repository's daily CSVs in analysis/data, originally downloaded with yfinance. This page uses the saved Close series without downloading or adjusting them again. The downloader did not explicitly set auto_adjust, so the field name alone does not establish the adjustment or dividend convention.

Full-period return = last Close / first Close − 1. Maximum drawdown = the minimum of Close / prior running high − 1. Recovery is the first trading day after the trough that regains the corresponding peak; “not recovered” means only through the end of the sample. Theme paths use equal initial allocations, then hold without rebalancing, unlike the original daily-rebalanced portfolio analysis.

Correlation uses Pearson's coefficient on aligned daily returns, each calculated as Close[t] / Close[t−1] − 1. All 250 daily changes are included. Pair-group averages weight every unique pair equally. The group-return coefficient instead correlates the average daily returns of the 12 AI and 10 semiconductor stocks, equivalent to daily equal-weight rebalancing before costs. This is distinct from the buy-and-hold theme paths in the overview. Rolling correlations use 30 or 60 daily changes and start only once a full window exists. Undefined correlations are shown as unavailable.

The companies reflect personal interests and selection bias; the theme groups are not comprehensive industry indices. Historical results are not forecasts. Cash examples scale the saved price series without fees, taxes or separately added cash dividends. Co-movement may reflect shared market influences; it does not establish a business cause or predictive edge.
